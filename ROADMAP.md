# Stream Find roadmap

## Shipped foundations

1. Autocomplete with artwork
2. Provider directory and logos
3. Season-level availability
4. Trailers and richer title details
5. Friend recommendations and shared watchlists
6. Live homepage discovery
7. Country selection
8. Watchlist release and availability alerts

Signed-in discovery, person profiles, taste signals, friend search, Activity, and push notifications extend this foundation.

## Current direction: make a viewing decision

- **Decide together:** an invited circle sets a mood and time, gets a short list based on included services and taste, votes, and chooses a title. Availability is calculated for the host's country; title pages show the current watch options.
- **Decide solo:** browse popular shows and movies on a selected service, or trending actors with paths into their filmographies. These are TMDB popularity and availability results, not streaming platforms' official charts.

## Next quality passes

1. Validate the group flow with multiple real accounts, including invitation, voting, sign-in return, and differing countries.
2. Improve ranking consistency across search, details, solo discovery, and group picks. Explain why a title is suggested and which subscriptions cover it.
3. Add provenance and freshness to suitability, reviewer, and popularity signals; make corrections easy to report.
4. Recheck music-video classification and the complete alert lifecycle across countries and notification states.
5. Iterate on friend-powered discovery and taste compatibility before expanding group decision tools.
